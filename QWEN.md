## Qwen Added Memories
- Project: Confaero App - A Flutter conference companion app built with GetX state management, GoRouter navigation, and Clean Architecture. Features include: Events, Agenda, Attendees, Community, Messages, Notifications, QR scanning, Job postings, Sponsors, Exhibitors, Floor maps, Live meetings, Polls, Documents, Photos, and Notes. Uses packages: go_router, get, flutter_screenutil, cached_network_image, syncfusion_flutter_charts, socket_io_client, qr_flutter, mobile_scanner, table_calendar, etc. Architecture: lib/core (config, constants, di, helpers), lib/features (auth, agenda, attendees, community, message, notification, events, onboarding, mainattendees, bottomNavbar), lib/presentations (controller, widgets), lib/global (assets). Entry point: main.dart with DevicePreview, ThemeController, NoInterNetScreen. Navigation: GoRouter with 50+ routes. Theme: Custom light/dark themes with AppColors. Network: connectivity_plus for connection monitoring.
